The backgrounds and contour geometry are fixed from saved 6000 × 6000 scans. Entropy is estimated for the continuous extension of the cubic to the compactified real line; it is not inferred from the Lyapunov exponents. Identical degree-64 kneading matrices share cached entropy estimates. Degree-32 comparisons and the calculation method are documented in the entropy notes. Orbit values are calculated locally at your exact selected (u, v), rather than at the nearest grid sample. Chaotic trajectories may differ from the GPU trajectories through rounding. Escape is reported only after crossing a parameter-dependent bound that guarantees continued growth.
